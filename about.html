About Amy Nance

Amy Nance, Therapist

Hello! I’m glad you’re here. If you’re reading this, chances are that you’re struggling. You are not alone. My way of coping with a challenging adolescent experience was to set a rigid trajectory that left no room for self-exploration. This led me down a career path that I did not enjoy, and I wasted years grappling with this truth.

Then, therapy changed my life! I learned the flexibility of mind to acknowledge what wasn’t working, and I finally gave myself permission to make a change. Soon after, I found my calling as a mental health counselor working with teens and young adults.

My approach is direct; I don’t beat around the bush. However, I am easy-going and fundamentally non-judgmental. I also hold a strong belief in the healing power of humor, so be prepared for a throw-my-head-back laugh that’s loud enough to be heard from space.

Rocket, Dog

Say hello to Rocket, therapy dog-in-training. He loves zoomies, a good chew and challenging you to a super serious game of tug-o’-war.

Full disclosure: he snores, he farts and he’s solar-powered like every Boston Terrier ever. He hopes to one day be the bestest behaved work boy, but right now he’s a puppy still learning manners including how not to lick your face off.

If that sounds like fun to you, great! He will happily accept booty scritches while snoozing on your lap. If not, he’s chill with napping in his crate while we talk.
