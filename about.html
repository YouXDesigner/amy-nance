About Amy Nance

Amy Nance, Therapist

Hello! I’m glad you’re here. If you’re reading this, chances are you’re struggling. You’re not alone. My way of coping with a challenging adolescent experience was to set a rigid trajectory that left no room for self-exploration. This led me down a career path I didn’t enjoy, and I wasted years grappling with this truth.

Then, therapy changed my life! I learned the flexibility of mind to acknowledge what wasn’t working and finally gave myself permission to make a change. Soon after, I found my calling as a mental health therapist working with teens and young adults.

My approach is direct; I don’t beat around the bush. But I’m easy-going and fundamentally non-judgmental. I also hold a strong belief in the healing power of humor, so be prepared for a laugh loud enough to be heard from space.

Rocket, Dog

Say hello to Rocket, therapy dog-in-training! This handsome guy loves zoomies, a good chew and super serious games of tug-o’-war. He’s also solar-powered and prefers snoozing in the sun or under a blanket on a warm lap. Rocket is in the office with me most days and adores meeting new people.

Full disclosure: like every Boston Terrier, he snores, farts and licks faces with pure abandon. He hopes to one day be the bestest behaved work boy, but right now he’s a puppy and still learning manners.

If that sounds like a welcome adventure, great! He will happily accept booty scritches while chilling next to you on the couch.

If not, he’s cool with napping in his crate while we talk.
