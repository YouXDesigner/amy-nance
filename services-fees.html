Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy is an evidence-based modality that teaches you how your thoughts, feelings and behaviors are connected. Together, you and I will identify the unhelpful patterns that are holding you back. Then we’ll practice coping skills to help you move forward.

The tools I give you will be tailored to your specific needs, because what works for someone else might not work for you. It’s not easy, but you get out what you put in. When you start to see change, you’ll know the work was worth it.

If that all sounds super clinical, don’t worry. I’m a real person who realizes you are also a real person, not a robot that needs to be re-programmed. So if you come in to the office and you’re just not feeling it that day, we can simply chat or enjoy the quiet over a game or art project.
