FAQ

What are your fees?

My fee is $160 per 50-minute session. I offer a free 15-minute phone consultation for prospective clients.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not currently accept insurance. In part, this is to protect your privacy and freedom to determine the course
of your own treatment. Insurance companies require you to prove, through the release of your private records
and diagnosis, how many sessions they will pay for, or if therapy is warranted at all. Many insurance companies
will reimburse a portion of the cost if you submit a superbill, which I can provide upon request.

Will we meet in person or online?

That’s up to you! I offer both telehealth and in-person sessions in Kirkland, Washington. You can choose on a
week-by-week basis how you’d prefer to meet. Just let me know at least 24 hours in advance if you plan to come
in to the office.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you need to cancel an appointment, please provide at least 48-hours notice. No-shows or sessions cancelled
with less than 48-hours notice will be charged at the full rate. If you reschedule your appointment within the same
week, you will not be charged a late-cancel or no-show fee.
