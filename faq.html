FAQ

What are your fees?

My fee is $160 per 50-minute session. I offer a free 15-minute phone consultation for prospective clients.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not currently accept insurance. In part, this is to protect your privacy and freedom to determine the course of your own treatment. Insurance companies require you to prove, through the release of your private records and diagnosis, that therapy is warranted. Often, they limit the number of sessions they will pay for.

I can, however, provide you with monthly superbills to submit for partial reimbursement. I encourage you to check with your insurance company beforehand to determine your rate of reimbursement.

Will we meet in person or online?

That’s up to you! I offer both telehealth and in-person sessions in Kirkland, WA. You can choose on a week-by-week basis how you’d prefer to meet. Just let me know at least 24 hours in advance if you plan to come in to the office.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you need to cancel an appointment, please provide at least 48-hours notice. No-shows or sessions cancelled with less than 48-hours notice will be charged at the full rate. If you reschedule your appointment within the same week, however, you will not be charged a late-cancel or no-show fee.
